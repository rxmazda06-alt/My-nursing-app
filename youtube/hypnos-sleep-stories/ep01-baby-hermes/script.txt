@CHAPTER 2 | Born at Sunrise
@SCENE s02_kyllene_dawn

N: [softly] Long, long ago, in a part of Greece called Arcadia, there was a mountain named Kyllene. [pause] And one morning, just as the sky turned from grey to pink to gold, Maia had a baby. A perfectly ordinary looking baby. Round cheeks. Tiny fists. H: [tired] Cute baby. Suspicious hair.

N: [softly] Maia wrapped him in soft swaddling cloths, the way you wrap babies, so they feel safe and snug. She laid him in a little cradle woven from willow branches. She kissed his forehead. H: [approvingly] Yes. Good. That's the correct move.

H: [sighs] Of course he didn't.

@CHAPTER 3 | The Great Cow Heist
@SCENE s03_pieria_sunset
