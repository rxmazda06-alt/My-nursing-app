A newborn god. Fifty stolen cows. One very tired god of sleep.

Tonight, Hypnos, the god of sleep, helps tell the story of baby Hermes, who was born at sunrise, invented the lyre by lunchtime, and stole fifty cows from his big brother Apollo before bedtime. Then he lied about it to Zeus with a perfectly straight face.

It's funny at the start and soft and sleepy by the end, with a gentle lyre lullaby to drift off to. You don't have to stay awake for the ending.
